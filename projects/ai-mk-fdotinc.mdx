## The Goal: Complete a Lap Without Breaking Reality

Your mission is simple but challenging: **complete one full lap around the track**.

Here's the catch: the AI only knows what it has seen before. If you steer too wildly or end up in unfamiliar territory, the model starts to lose track of reality. The world begins to blur and warp like a dream, and you might find yourself teleported to completely different parts of the track... or even off it entirely!

Stay smooth, stay in context, and see if you can make it to the finish line.

## Tips & Things to Watch For

Here are some fascinating behaviors you'll notice while playing:

### 🌀 **Infinite Turns**

Sometimes when you're turning, the AI gets stuck in a loop. Each new frame looks so similar to the last that the model thinks the turn never ends! The track just keeps curving forever.

**Pro tip**: Add some variation to break the loop, alternate between slight left and right movements, or try the reset button.

### 🚇 **The Endless Tunnel**

The tunnel section is a repeating pattern, which makes it easy for the AI to just... never show you the exit. You might drive through the same tunnel segments infinitely.

**Escape strategy**: Similar to turns: add some steering variation to break the pattern.

### 🔄 **Time Travel**

Here's a wild one: the section after the tunnel looks very similar to the track's starting area. Sometimes the AI gets confused and teleports you back to the beginning, even when you're almost at the finish line!

It's like the neural network is having déjà vu.

### 🍄 **Boost Mechanics**

The model learned that boost only works when you have a mushroom power-up (look for the icon at the top of the screen). Try using boost when you have one—and remember to **hold the F key down** so the AI can process the boost animation across multiple frames.

**Bonus**: Sometimes hitting reset will start you from a frame where you already have boost available!
